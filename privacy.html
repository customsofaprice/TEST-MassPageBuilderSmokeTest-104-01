Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Desert Buggy Rental Dubai ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit this website or get in touch with us. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your information responsibly.

Information we collect

We collect two kinds of information:

  • Information you give us — such as your name, phone number, email address, and any details you include when you contact us or request a quote.
  • Information collected automatically — like most websites, our server and analytics tools may record basic technical data your browser sends, such as your approximate location, IP address, device and browser type, and the pages you view.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • respond to your enquiries and provide the services you ask about;
  • operate, maintain, and improve this website;
  • keep our site secure and prevent abuse; and
  • meet any legal or regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and log files

Like most websites, this site may use cookies and standard server log files. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help the site function and help us understand how it is used. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site may then work differently.

Analytics and third-party services

We may use privacy-respecting analytics services (for example, Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. These providers may set their own cookies and process data under their own privacy policies. We do not control, and are not responsible for, the privacy practices of third-party websites we link to.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to or restrict how we use it (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR). If you are a California resident, you may request that we do not sell or share your personal information (under the CCPA). To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below and we will respond as required by law.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
